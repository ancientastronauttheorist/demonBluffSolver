chronological service ledgers, raw arguments/callers, native graph, queue
admissions and final CPU for both emulators. Every Manage service includes
inherited CLR/Unity providers and engine gateways. Each attempted invocation

tree order. Earlier completed payloads and physical owner storage are retained

stable checkpoints; intermediate insertion prefixes retain their partial

all-service paused/reentered corpus is distinct from independent aborts. It
