## Existing evidence and the public-domain blocker

The [generation/pool catalogue audit](first_village_bluff_generation.md) starts
from original profile `21674`, whose five starting Villagers are Confessor,
Lover, Hunter, Enlightened and Gemcrafter, with ordinary Minion `21596`.
Standard selects four Villagers. The retained row-zero witness has physical
current data `[Minion, Confessor, Lover, Hunter, Enlightened]`, duplicate pool
`[Confessor, Lover, Hunter, Enlightened]`, and unique pool
`[Gemcrafter, Alchemist]`. Its [retained acquisition](first_village_retained_acquisition.md)
selects Confessor through the recorded duplicate branch. The
[non-Day Rust boundary](non_day_setup_reveal.md) separately supports all six
copied callbacks; it does not certify six retained native full histories.

That fixed native roster is privileged validation evidence. It cannot establish
the player's unknown base roster, other seats' roles, or the Minion's copied
role. Neither a domain string nor a trusted review of one Hunter sentence
establishes those facts. The catalogue audit's generation, sort, pool and
selector factors do not certify every joint `120 permutations x 6 bluffs`
history, and their counts are not weights.

Ordinary unique acquisition registers its selected role into the current
script's faction list; duplicate acquisition does not. In the row-zero
conditional family, duplicate choices leave four current Villager entries,
whereas Gemcrafter/Alchemist unique choices add a fifth. Thus a complete,
current public deck partitions those six branches. It must not be replaced by
the six-entry hidden selector pool or interpreted as four/five physical Good
cards: the HUD still describes four Villagers and one Minion on five cards.

The partition itself requires the actual Shuffle-to-deck-update publication
and subscriber chronology. A stale pre-acquisition deck or a partial pinned
strip cannot establish the current public list. The read-only discovery of an
original PinnedDeckView subscriber and its null/timed yields led to a private
retained composition: installed subscriber, Audio/Shuffle continuation and
normal/pause parity now execute on the same graph. A conditional native Hunter
result also completes under supplied Day/input fields. The
[continuation checkpoint](../../SOLVER_CONTRACT.md) records these bounded
results and remaining validation obligations. Pinned ShowAll omits Villagers;
complete modal DeckView publication, original Day and trusted public capture
remain open. This is not a readiness or legal-history certificate. Existing
[deck visibility](deck_view_visibility.md) and
[DeckCharacter registration](deck_character_registration.md) audits establish
bounded callers under supplied services, not this complete publication.

Across the original profile, the same public deck can correspond to other
omitted-base-role histories. For example, observing all five original Villager
names does not identify which four were physical Good cards before acquisition.
The wider fallback catalogue has 24 candidates. An implementation must retain
every alternative admitted by its declared public family, or reject that
family explicitly; it cannot quietly reuse row zero or prune to six candidates.

The five native Standard subsets are the five choices of an omitted starting
Villager; four contain a real Gemcrafter. The older setup-action V2 and
acquisition V5 lack that real data binding. The opt-in
[Gemcrafter boundary](non_day_gemcrafter_setup.md) adds setup-action V3,
acquisition V6 and scheduled acquisition V3, using exact inherited selectors
and shared non-Day callback bodies. This removes that class-binding gap under
declared authored inputs; it does not certify the other four original retained
generation histories. Both acquisition versions still reject an entire selector
pool containing any of the other 18 fallback catalogue roles, even when a
particular selected duplicate role is supported. Their six-role allowlist is
a callback boundary, not a replacement for the native 24-role catalogue.

A public four-entry deck does not eliminate unobserved fallback choices.
Duplicate acquisition leaves that deck unchanged for every fallback candidate;
a unique selection already present in the current roster also suppresses an
addition. The omitted starting Villager and selected fallback remain ordered
unique-pool occurrences, including possible equal identities. The row-zero
two-entry pool `[Gemcrafter, Alchemist]` is a conditional offline input. Its
Alchemist restriction cannot be inferred from the player's current deck.

## Smallest proposed public input

Reuse the strict [PlayerHistory v1 boundary](player_history_boundary.md) with a
new, externally reviewed conditional domain, provisionally
`conditional_initial_day_first_village_minion_hunter_v1`. Current
[player_history.rs](../../../crates/solver-core/src/player_history.rs)
supports only Judge and Hunter/Baa projection domains and rejects this new name.
No schema field carrying oracle roles, source pointers, selector draws, bluff
identity or self-certified visibility is proposed.

The first admissible prefix needs:

- One `DeckObserved` after the actual relevant deck publication, preserving
  every exposed public role occurrence and faction. Its complete current deck
  must be tied to the appropriate registration/publication branch. An obscured
  or partial list is outside this proposed initial slice.
- Reviewed visible HUD counts: `n_cards=5`, `n_evil=1`, Villagers `4`, Outcasts
  `0`, Minions `1`, Demons `0`. Current HUD provenance stays current;
  `TrustedPreStart` is not inferred from an offline native setup.
- A reviewed initial Day checkpoint with actual public HP and established
  wrong-execution cost. Engine queue mask `2` and body state Hidden `5` do not
  establish global Day, public availability or legal clicking.
- One serial, legally admitted reveal request and its actual completion, bound
  to the same displayed seat. The completed `CardRevealed` has apparent role
  `Hunter`, exact speech, `rule_version=public_current`, and `targets=[]`.
  Native ActedInfo reference IDs and their order remain validation-only.

Physical indices `0..4` map to displayed labels `[5,4,3,2,1]` in the original
retained witness. Public event positions are reviewed displayed labels. A
separate verified geometry/identity mapping must establish the circle; native
actor pointers never become player-history positions. The four other seats
receive no invented `CardInfo`, role, clue, status or revealed flag.

The exact Hunter strings are `I am 1 card away from closest Evil` and
`I am N cards away from closest Evil`, with canonical decimal `N` and plural
for values other than one. For N5, native syntax support is `{1,2,4}`;
distance `3`, leading zeros, wrong singular/plural and altered wording are
unsupported. Missing role/text/deck/HP/cost evidence is incomplete.

These rules come from the [Hunter native note](../roles/gameplay_roles_scout_hunter.md),
[concrete Day publication](hunter_role_publication.md) and
[conditional acquisition/publication join](hunter_acquisition_publication.md).
Those joins do not yet establish this original mixed N5 first legal reveal.
The [capture-admission obligations](hunter_capture_admission.md) still require
exact event/prefix review, build/capture identity and actual publicly exposed
role/text/deck/chronology; solver-ready native text is insufficient.

## Exact reference and the legacy quotient

For a **separately declared conditional model**, independently enumerate all
admitted latent base-role placements and copied-role alternatives, then filter
by the public deck branch and the one Hunter observation. Derive family sizes
from that enumeration rather than treating a native support-row count as a
complete-world count. Ordinary profile reachability and runtime services remain
explicit dependencies of that conditional model.

A canonical latent-world key must preserve the complete per-seat original/current
role, runtime alignment and copied role, deterministic status/resistance/target
state, and registered current deck occurrence multiset. Preserve any acquisition
history distinctions that affect later transitions. Real Confessor's appearance
status `25` and a Minion copying Confessor's status `25` do not make that Minion
truthful; copied Alchemist does not receive real Alchemist's Init resistance.
Hidden values belong only to reference hypotheses and the oracle comparison.

Status `25` is `AppearTruthfull`. The
[native Confessor contract](../roles/gameplay_role_confessor.md) separates this
appearance from registered alignment: `GetRegisterAlignment` uses a Unity-live
register-as record's starting alignment, otherwise the actor's stored alignment,
without reading statuses. All five retained row-zero actors have null register-as.
Real Confessor therefore remains registered Good, and its Minion copy remains
registered Evil. Under this declared boundary Hunter still sees only the Minion
as registered Evil; other register-as records or intervening writers require
their own transition model.

Fresh status assumptions must be declared. Ordinary
[Character.Init](../../../crates/solver-core/src/bluff/character_initialization.rs) clears the
active status list and runtime pointer, but preserves resistance and the shared
status target. The setup bridge preserves those fields. Empty resistance and
null target are supplied fresh-boundary conditions, not consequences of profile
21674. Under those conditions, real Confessor adds 25 during Init, copied
Confessor adds 25 to the Minion during acquisition, and repeated accepted 25
clears the target without inserting another list entry. Preserve the supplied
initial version and exact insertion increments; do not treat version zero as a
native asset property. The other admitted Init/AfterRoundStart callbacks leave
runtime data null. Uninitialized Enlightened Day data must not be populated with
an invented direction before an actual Day callback.

Before the legal-native join, the smallest feasible independent reference is
therefore an **offline, input-conditioned setup/acquisition support reference**:
the declared four-Good subset omits Gemcrafter; its ordered duplicate pool is
those four roles; its ordered unique pool is exactly Gemcrafter/Alchemist;
must-include is empty; source/clone classes match; resistance/target and initial
versions are explicit; there are no subscribers, statuses from other writers or
intervening mutations. Given each explicitly supplied seat placement and order,
independently derive the four duplicate and two unique semantic outcomes. Check
complete actor roles, runtime alignment, copied role, register-as, ordered
statuses/resistance/target, Start latch, bodies, current data/order, registration
and pool state against setup V2 and acquisition V5. Queue identity, pending
records and deferred Audio/Shuffle require the separately supplied V2 queue
boundary. Support equality ignores supplied RNG weights; selector occurrence
and branch chronology remain separate from deduplicated semantic worlds.

This can test conditional semantic equality without asserting a Cartesian
native-history certificate or admitting a public belief domain. A first-Hunter
reference may subsequently filter that explicitly conditional support using the
exact circle/text rule, but global Day, click legality and public publication
remain additional prerequisites. Whole-profile or whole-fallback full-world
equality remains unsupported until the remaining copied callbacks, original
retained Gemcrafter histories, retained state and reachable caller contracts
are represented and independently compared.
Their opaque effects must not be replaced with inert callbacks. No new combined
world count or generation prior is established by this proposal.

The independent Hunter rule walks the verified circle in both directions,
excludes the actor, and uses the nearest registered Evil or sentinel `N-1`.
Here a real Good Hunter sees the sole ordinary Minion at distance `1` or `2`.
A Minion appearing as Hunter has self-excluded truth `4`, remains lying, and
can emit either `1` or `2`. Consequently exact distance `4` is valid native
syntax but a contradiction within this fully declared one-Evil mixed model;
it yields an empty complete conditional world set, not a parser error.

The legacy [GameState/Scenario](../../../crates/solver-core/src/types.rs) and
[scenario builder](../../../crates/solver-core/src/scenario.rs) do not represent
all latent Good-role placements and Minion bluff alternatives in this family.
For one Hunter clue, compare their output only to an explicitly named deduction
quotient: possible Minion seats, derived **after public-deck filtering**. In the
row-zero four-Villager duplicate-deck family, distance `1`/`2` permits the
revealed seat as fake Hunter plus the two seats at the claimed distance. In
the row-zero family with a registered fifth Gemcrafter/Alchemist entry, the
copied role is that unique addition, so apparent Hunter must be Good and only
the two geometric seats remain. Broader public families require their own
complete filtering. If legacy production cannot preserve those deck-registration
constraints, that projection remains unsupported; it must not force the
three-seat result onto every family.
Keep the full reference set separately. Equal Evil-seat quotients do not prove
equal complete worlds, future transition support or probabilities.

## Finite exit and next S2 integration

After the original legal trace and trusted public prefix exist, predeclare the
supported public deck families and independently enumerate their full latent
conditional worlds. For every supported single Hunter reveal, require complete
canonical reference equality and a separately labeled production Evil-seat
quotient equality. Include empty contradiction sets, exact syntax rejection,
missing-evidence errors, deck-registration/delta mismatches and unsupported
richer families. Require paired hidden worlds with identical legal public
histories to produce identical admitted/planner inputs and deductions.

Reject unreviewed event prefixes, privileged/unknown fields, native target
references, repeated reveals, unsupported roles/events, Night/reset transitions
and HP changes outside the declared initial slice. Do not derive policy weights
from native RNG draws, support cardinalities or the recorded Confessor path.

The next S2 solver change should expose a typed mixed-domain belief result that
retains canonical latent alternatives and explicit unsupported/incomplete states,
then compare it to the independent reference. A legacy snapshot may remain a
compatibility/deduction projection. Legal-action transitions, priors/bounds and
policy evaluation follow only after their own contracts; UI dependency closure
alone does not complete the solver-first goal.
